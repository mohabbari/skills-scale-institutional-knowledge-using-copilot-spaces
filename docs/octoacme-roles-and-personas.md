# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads design and execute quality assurance strategies, define testing approaches, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and QA strategy aligned with project scope
- Define acceptance criteria in collaboration with Product Managers
- Design and execute manual and automated test cases
- Identify defects and validate fixes
- Establish quality gates and exit criteria for releases
- Report on test coverage and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Maintain and improve test coverage over time

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria refinement
- Test status updates in standups and weekly syncs
- Defect logs and quality reports
- Release readiness assessments

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Collaborate on test scenarios, review code testability, validate fixes
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria, validate feature behavior
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality metrics, flag release blockers

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical design decisions, system architecture, and integration strategies. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical vision.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical proposals and approve design approaches
- Define architecture and integration patterns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Provide technical guidance and mentoring to developers
- Evaluate technology choices and trade-offs
- Participate in code reviews for complex or critical changes

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure technical solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Enable team productivity through clear technical direction
- Support long-term product vision with sound architecture

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback on complex changes
- Risk identification and mitigation planning
- Technology evaluation and recommendation docs

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Guide design decisions, mentor, review code
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Flag technical risks and dependencies

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests, provide requirements, and make key decisions. They ensure projects deliver value and align with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project scope and resource allocation
- Provide business context and priority guidance
- Review progress and make go/no-go decisions
- Support escalations and remove organizational blockers
- Communicate outcomes to broader business

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy
- Maintain visibility and control over key initiatives
- Support team success through timely decisions and support

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status reports
- Decision gates and milestone reviews
- Escalation and issue resolution

### Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Provide context, review status, approve changes
- **With Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and success metrics
- **With Developers and QA**: Attend demos and provide feedback

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security and regulatory requirements. They provide guidance on data protection, compliance frameworks, and risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Review architecture and design for security implications
- Conduct security assessments and compliance audits
- Provide guidance on data protection and privacy standards
- Identify and mitigate security risks
- Track compliance with regulatory requirements and industry standards

### Goals
- Ensure projects meet security and regulatory requirements
- Reduce security incidents and compliance violations
- Build security awareness and best practices across the team
- Maintain trust with customers and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews during planning
- Risk assessment and mitigation discussions
- Compliance audit reports and status updates
- Incident response and post-incident reviews

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Review code and architecture for security risks, provide secure coding guidance
- **With Technical Leads**: Advise on secure design patterns and technology choices
- **With Project Managers**: Flag security and compliance risks, ensure security gates are met before release

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, release operations, and system reliability. They enable teams to deploy with confidence and maintain system health.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines
- Automate testing, building, and deployment processes
- Monitor system performance, availability, and reliability
- Support incident response and post-incident reviews
- Document infrastructure and deployment procedures
- Collaborate on capacity planning and infrastructure scaling

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and safe deployments
- Minimize deployment failures and downtime
- Reduce manual operations and toil
- Maintain system performance and security

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- Infrastructure and performance metrics reports
- Incident response and post-incident analysis
- CI/CD pipeline improvements and automation updates

### Interactions
- **With Developers**: Support build and deployment questions, provide deployment guidance
- **With QA/Testing Leads**: Collaborate on staging environment setup and smoke testing
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate release timing, report on deployment readiness

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach (Optional)

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They enable the team to self-organize and continuously improve.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove team impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Monitor team velocity and process health
- Support backlog refinement and prioritization
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and delivery cadence
- Improve team collaboration and communication
- Enable continuous process improvement
- Maintain team morale and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- One-on-one coaching and mentoring
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Support planning and scheduling, escalate process impediments
- **With All Team Members**: Facilitate collaboration, remove blockers, coach on agile practices
